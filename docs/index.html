The Business Problem

Three questions leadership couldn't answer

The organization of 1,470 employees had above-benchmark attrition but no view of what it cost, whether pay rewarded performance, or who would leave next.

01 · COST

What is turnover really costing us?

Headcount reports showed 237 exits but not the dollar impact, or which losses actually hurt.

02 · FAIRNESS

Do we actually pay for performance?

Top performers got bigger raises, yet they left just as often. Something in the pay structure was broken.

03 · RISK

Who is about to leave?

HR reacted to resignations after the fact. They needed a ranked list of at-risk talent to act on now.
